A selection of projects from the lab, pulled from our [GitHub](https://github.com/IBSS-lab).

### [Food Exchange System](https://github.com/IBSS-lab/cu_food_exchange)
A nutrition-planning web app that calculates equivalent amounts of foods with matching calorie, carb, protein, or fat content, based on the food exchange list from Thanyarak Pattani Hospital. Node.js/Express/MongoDB backend with a no-build-step HTML/CSS/JS frontend, deployed on Vercel. [Live demo](https://cufoodexchange.vercel.app).

### [Dip Coater Firmware](https://github.com/IBSS-lab/Dip_Coater)
Arduino firmware for a stepper-motor-driven dip coater, controlling the sample stage over serial with single-character commands (up/down/stop) using the AccelStepper library, with a built-in auto-stop safety timer against over-travel.
